📖 Bar Charades – Game Guide

Bar Charades is a team-based acting game with bar-themed prompts. Teams take turns acting out prompts silently while teammates race to guess the word — all against the clock.

How to Play

  1. Set up 2–4 teams and choose a turn duration (30–90 seconds).
  2. The active team's actor taps Reveal Prompt & Start Timer when ready.
  3. Act out the prompt silently — no words, no sounds!
  4. Teammates shout guesses; tap ✓ Correct! when they get it right.
  5. Tap ✗ Pass to skip a prompt (costs a point). Time running out also costs a point.
  6. Teams rotate; the team with the most points after all rounds wins.

Bonus Round

Watch for the ✨ Double Trouble banner — these special prompts are worth 2 points if guessed correctly!
